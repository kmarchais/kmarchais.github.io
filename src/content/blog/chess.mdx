## Why a periodic board

Most chess variants change the pieces. Periodic chess changes the topology
instead: the board is a cylinder. The a-file and the h-file are adjacent,
so a rook on a1 attacks h1 directly, and a bishop slides off one side and
re-enters on the other.

That single change cascades into the rest of the game:

- Knights gain new wrap-around squares
- Discovered checks become harder to anticipate
- Endgames where one side has only a king become winning for the other side
  much more often, because the king can no longer hide in a corner
