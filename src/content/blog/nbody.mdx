## Direct summation, in your browser

The naïve O(N²) N-body solver is embarrassingly parallel: every body's
acceleration is a sum over every other body. With WebGPU compute, each
workgroup loads a tile of source bodies into shared memory, then every
thread in the workgroup accumulates contributions from that tile against
its assigned target body. Repeat for every tile and you have the full
force sum.

This article will walk through:

- The leapfrog integrator and why it's symplectic
- Plummer softening so close encounters don't blow up
- Workgroup tiling and the storage-barrier dance
- Plummer / cold-collapse / cube initial conditions
- A playable demo with sliders for G, softening, and damping
