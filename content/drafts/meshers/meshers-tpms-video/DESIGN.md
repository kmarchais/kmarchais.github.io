# Meshers video identity

## Style prompt
Use the existing Meshers article's quiet technical style. A large teal TPMS surface fills a dark cell, with warm brass on exposed tetrahedron faces. Start with the shape already visible. Use a smooth field morph and a slow camera move. The actual Gyroid connectivity is the final subject. All copy must remain legible at phone size.

## Colors
- Ground: #0b1a20
- Ink: #edf2ec
- Muted labels: #a1b8bc
- Mesh: #79c8bc
- Brass: #d9ac72

## Typography
Archivo Black for short display lines, IBM Plex Mono for technical labels. Both are bundled by Hyperframes. Use the article palette and a heavy display face against restrained data labels.

## What not to do
No neon bloom, generic particle backgrounds, simulated benchmark clocks, unsourced performance claims or raw-number tables. The TPMS morph is explicitly an illustration. Only the final Gyroid is an actual Meshers volume mesh.
